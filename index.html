Project-2 Executive Dashboard Development

Reliance Industries Limited
Dashboard development required translating raw metrics into key performance indicators. We organized the development process into four structured stages.
1 Data Auditing

Audited and cleaned raw division performance data, verifying metrics against target business values.

2 KPI Definition

Selected key operational metrics, including fixed cost variance, EBITDA, safety events, and plant efficiency index.

3 Worksheet Design

Created individual Tableau worksheets, designing custom radial performance dials and status trackers.

4 Sheet Integration

Assembled the worksheets into a single dashboard layout, organizing sections to present a clear division scorecard.

Presenter Contributions:

Cleaned the Division Scorecard dataset, designed worksheets, calculated target variances, and laid out the executive dashboard. (No database admin or server deployment tasks were claimed).

Challenges, Solutions & Key Learnings

RelianceIndustries Limited
Key challenges and how I addressed them
Databricks Learning

Challenge: Learning a new analytics environment. Response: Used training material and practised queries.

Fair AI Comparison

Challenge: Outputs varied in style and length. Response: Used common prompts and evaluation criteria.

Incomplete Data

Challenge: Some scorecard fields were empty. Response: Used calculated fields and representative averages for selected visuals.

Custom Tableau Gauges

Challenge: Semi-circular gauges were not native. Response: Used trigonometric calculations to position marks along an arc.

Technical Growth

Practised Databricks SQL, data validation, Tableau calculated fields, prompt design and structured AI evaluation.

Business & Professional Learning

Learned to connect IT tools with business questions, select relevant KPIs and communicate findings clearly for management review.

Slide 9: Challenges, Solutions & Key Learnings

The main challenges were learning Databricks, maintaining consistency when comparing AI assistants, handling incomplete scorecard fields and creating custom gauges in Tableau. I addressed them through practice, common prompts and criteria, calculated fields and trigonometric formulas. The experience strengthened my technical skills and helped me connect analytics outputs to business needs.
